The Jacquard Loom

This plate is not stored as an image. It is a chain of punched cards for Joseph Marie Jacquard's loom of 1804, woven one pass of the shuttle at a time. Each letter is a figure where the warp lies on top of the cloth.

The ground is a five-end satin with the weft on top, and the letters are the same satin turned over so the warp shows. The speed follows H. William Nelson's Jacquard Machines (1909), which gives ninety to a hundred and thirty picks a minute for a single-action machine. In 1839 Michel-Marie Carquillat wove a silk portrait of Jacquard from some 24,000 cards, and Babbage liked to show his copy to guests.
